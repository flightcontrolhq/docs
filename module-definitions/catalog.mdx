---
title: "Module catalog"
description: "The Ravion module catalog hosts reusable infrastructure modules, with definitions, versions, and instances that teams provision on demand."
---

The catalog holds module **definitions**. Each definition has **versions**, and a version is instantiated as a module **instance** in an environment.

By default, your catalog includes the Ravion standard library of production ready modules. These modules have type prefix of `rvn-*` and have `organizationId=null`.

You can add your own module definitions to the catalog. Your modules are only accessible by your organization.

<Note>
  We will be adding ability to remove our standard library modules for teams that want to completely
  control their own catalog. Reach out if you need this and we can prioritize it.
</Note>
