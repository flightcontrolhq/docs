Ravion is built on two primitives: **modules** and **pipelines**. Everything else — organizations, projects, and environments — exists to organize and scope them.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Modules" icon="cube" href="/concepts/modules">A deployable unit of infrastructure with optional stack, build, deploy, metrics, logs, and links.</Card>
  <Card title="Pipelines" icon="diagram-project" href="/concepts/pipelines">A DAG of steps that builds, provisions, and deploys your modules.</Card>
</CardGroup>
