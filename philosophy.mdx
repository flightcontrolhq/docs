---
title: "Product philosophy"
description: "Ravion gives developers and agents self-service while keeping platform and DevOps teams in control."
---

Ravion is built around a simple belief: developers and agents should move fast, and platform and DevOps engineers should still control how cloud infrastructure is provisioned, deployed, and operated.

Most teams want to reduce DevOps overhead without giving up safety. PaaS tools are fast until you need deeper control. Raw IaC gives control, but it asks every developer to understand platform internals. A patchwork of deployment, observability, and infrastructure tools slows everyone down.

Ravion is the middle path: developer cloud self-service backed by Terraform, reusable modules, controlled pipelines, and shared visibility.
